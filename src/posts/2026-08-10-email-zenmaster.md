I write a lot of email, and I have opinions about how it should read. Clear, kind, direct, and free of the habits that make writing sound generated.

Email Zenmaster adds four things to Gmail, and each one only runs when I click it.

- A sparkle beside the subject field suggests subject lines.
- Selecting text brings up Zenify, which rewrites the selection in place with an Undo.
- A right-click review reads the whole thread and flags up to three real issues, starting with anything I'm repeating and any question I haven't answered.
- A right-click draft writes a reply based on the thread above.

## Writing like a person

The interesting part is what it won't do. Everything it generates is checked against a list of AI tells borrowed from my Is it AI? experiment. Stock openers, "not just X" contrasts, words like delve and robust, and punctuation habits I've banned from my own writing. If the model slips, it gets one corrected pass with the problem named.

It also leaves good writing alone. Early versions rewrote everything they touched. Now, if a passage is already fine, Zenify says there's nothing to change and keeps its hands off.

## What I'm watching

The number that matters is how often a rewrite gets undone. A tool that changes your words is only useful if the change survives the next ten seconds.
