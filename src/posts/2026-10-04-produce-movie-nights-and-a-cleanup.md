## Fresh produce, picked up from my driveway

I organized a neighborhood produce pickup service to help residents, including seniors, access farm-fresh food safely. They pulled up, opened the trunk, and I loaded a box of produce.

## Sixteen weeks of neighborhood movie nights

I produced a weekly drive-in movie series during the pandemic, giving neighbors a way to enjoy an evening together while keeping their distance.

## A monthly cleanup that’s still going six years later

Our neighborhood sits at the intersection of three jurisdictions. I started a monthly cleanup to address the gaps in maintenance and bring neighbors together around a shared responsibility.
